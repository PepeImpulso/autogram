# v1.18.5 — Tap-to-record, tap-to-send on the AA home 🎙️

Before: tapping Merlin in AA navigated to a separate "Grabando…"
screen with a "Parar y enviar" button. Users had to look at the
screen twice to send one message.

User asked for press-and-hold-release semantics ("grabar pulsando
sobre las imágenes y enviar automáticamente al soltar"). AA's
`SurfaceCallback` only emits `onClick` — no press/release events
are exposed, Google enforces that for driver-distraction safety.
The closest car-safe analogue is **tap-to-start / tap-to-stop on
the same surface, no navigation**.

## ✨ Changed

- **Recording stays on the home surface.** Previously
  `onGetTemplate` swapped to `MessageTemplate` when the send
  state flipped to `Recording`. Now we keep the
  `NavigationTemplate` so the custom canvas stays live
  throughout the recording.
- **Tap-to-stop-and-send anywhere on the surface.** While a
  recording is in flight, any `onClick` — on Merlin, on a
  secondary, on empty background — fires `stopAndSend`
  instead of hit-testing regions. Matches the muscle memory
  of "tap to finish".
- **Visual recording cue**: the active target's gold ring
  swaps to bright red and thickens from 4–5% → 8% of the
  circle radius, so which circle is recording reads at a
  glance. A red pill banner at the top of the surface reads
  **"Grabando · Toca para enviar"** (localized in all 6
  locales) so the tap-to-send affordance isn't hidden.

## 🏗 Architecture

- `AutogramSurfaceRenderer` gained an `isRecording` +
  `activeTargetId` pair of `@Volatile` fields, pushed via a
  new `updateRecordingState(Boolean, String?)` method from
  `MainScreen`. The renderer mixes these into `drawAvatar`'s
  ring colour + stroke, and into the top banner drawer.
- `MainScreen` observes both `sendMessageUseCase.status` and
  `sendMessageUseCase.activeTargetId` and fan-ins the state
  to the renderer.
- New `onStopAndSend` callback on the renderer, wired to
  `stopAndSend()` in `MainScreen`. Invoked from
  `onClick(x, y)` when `isRecording == true`, short-
  circuiting the normal region hit-test.

## Install

Update from 1.18.4. In AA:
1. Tap Merlin (or any other circle) → its ring turns red,
   the top banner shows "Grabando · Toca para enviar".
2. Speak.
3. Tap **anywhere** on the surface to stop and send.
