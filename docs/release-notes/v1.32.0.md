# v1.32.0 — Clean starts, Waze-friendly playback and a reply card in the car 🚗

This release fixes the clipped start of a message after you record a reply,
stops Autogram from talking over Waze / Maps prompts, and puts the last
contact who wrote to you on Android Auto's Now Playing card with a Reply
button.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Messages no longer lose their first second or two after you record
  (#4).** The 2-second pause after recording only applied to messages that
  were already queued: the recording gate was released *before* that pause,
  so a message arriving right after the mic closed started straight away,
  while the audio route was still switching back from capture, and its
  start was swallowed. The gate now opens a 2-second quiet window; anything
  that arrives inside it waits for the window to end, and a second
  recording during the wait restarts the full beat.
- **The start of a message is no longer swallowed by the car's head unit
  (#4).** A car only opens its audio channel once data flows, so the first
  words were eaten while it did. In the car, voice messages now start muted
  for 0.7 s and then rewind to the beginning, and TTS speaks 0.7 s of
  silence first. On the phone nothing changes.
- **Autogram no longer talks over Waze / Maps prompts (#2).** When a
  navigation app asked us to duck, we kept playing at full volume: Android
  never ducks speech content automatically, so the prompt was masked. Voice
  messages and TTS now pause while the prompt plays and continue from where
  they stopped when audio focus comes back (or after 15 s at most, in case
  the car never hands it back). Pausing rather than lowering the volume
  means you do not miss words of either.

## ✨ Added

- **Last sender on the Android Auto Now Playing card (#3).** When nothing is
  playing or recording, the card (including the compact dashboard tile next
  to the map) shows the last contact who wrote to you, with their photo, or
  their initial when there is none. Play plays that message, and the new
  **Reply** button starts a voice reply to that conversation, with the
  same tap-to-send / Cancel flow as the contact grid. The reply goes to the
  contact shown on the card when you tapped it, never to someone who wrote
  in the meantime. After sending, the card comes back 1.5 s later, so the
  car can still drop back to the grid first. It is only published while the
  phone is connected to a car. Android Auto decides where the Reply button
  goes on each screen: on narrow layouts it may end up in the overflow menu.

## 🧪 Tests

- Quiet-window tests: a message arriving after recording is queued, one
  arriving after the window plays at once, and a recording that ends while
  the drain is waiting restarts the full 2 s.
- The car lead-in applies to car connections and projected outputs only,
  never to phone playback.
- Contact-initial fallback for the artwork (emoji, blank names).

## Install

Update through Autogram's in-app updater. No new permissions or settings.
Please try the three fixes in the car: send a reply while a message is
arriving, play a message with Waze guiding you, and use the card's Reply
button from the dashboard. These behaviours depend on the head unit and
could not be tested on a real car before release.
