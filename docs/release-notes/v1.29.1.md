# v1.29.1 — Back to the car grid after sending 🚗↩️

First car test of v1.29.0: the experimental grid buttons did not show up,
and after sending, the car stayed on a "Sent" player screen instead of
going back to the contacts.

## 🔍 Changed

- **No "Sent" screen in the car.** After a car recording is sent, Autogram
  clears its player instead of showing "Sent to …". That gives Android Auto
  nothing to show in Now Playing so it can go back to the Grabar grid, where
  the contact's tile shows "Sent". Whether Android Auto actually leaves Now
  Playing on its own is up to Android Auto and needs a car test; a failed
  send still shows "Failed" and a queued one still shows "Queued".

## ✨ Added

- **Settings shows whether your car supports the grid buttons.** Under
  "Car grid buttons (experimental)", Autogram now shows how many buttons per
  contact Android Auto said it can draw on its last connection, or that it
  does not support them. If it says it does not, the grid buttons can never
  appear on that car, whatever the setting.

## Install

Update through Autogram's in-app updater, then connect to Android Auto once
so the support line in Settings is filled in.
