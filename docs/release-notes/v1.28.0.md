# v1.28.0 — Car record grid closer to the phone 🚗🎙️

Feedback from the first test drive of v1.27.0: tapping a contact jumped to
a separate player screen, sending meant hunting for a small button, and the
car grid didn't look like the phone. This release brings the "Grabar" tab
closer to the phone's Home screen.

## ✨ Added

- **Record without leaving the grid.** Each contact tile has a mic button.
  Tap it to start recording: the contact's photo gets a red stop badge, the
  tile says "● Recording — tap ➤ to send", and the mic button turns into
  send ➤ and cancel ✕. Tap ➤ to send. You stay on the grid the whole time.
  Tapping the photo itself still works, but Android Auto always opens its
  player for that, where the big ⏸ button sends.
- **Shortcuts in the car.** The phone's shortcuts (Correo, Proactive Task,
  Mejora, Tiempo, ...) appear as tiles after the contacts. Tap the tile's ➤
  button to send without leaving the grid. A shortcut with a contact goes to
  that contact; the rest go to the primary contact, like on the phone. They
  follow the phone's "show shortcuts" setting.
- **Unread counters on the photos.** Each contact shows the same pink
  pending-message counter as the phone.

## 🔍 Changed

- **Same contacts and order as the phone.** The grid shows the primary
  contact first, then the contacts visible on the phone's Home. Contacts
  hidden from Home are hidden in the car too.
- **Voice messages can be up to 3 minutes long** (was 1 minute), on the
  phone and in the car.
- **No car replies to approval requests.** Like the car home screen,
  recording or sending a shortcut is ignored while that contact's newest
  message is an approval request; approve it in Telegram.

## Install

Update through Autogram's in-app updater. The in-grid buttons need a recent
Android Auto app on the phone; if your Android Auto doesn't support them,
the tiles work as before (tap the photo, then ⏸ to send).
