# v1.28.1 — Car grid hotfix 🩹

v1.28.0 left the Autogram media screen in the car without contacts. This
hotfix turns off the change most likely responsible and keeps the rest of
v1.28.0.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Contacts are back on the car's "Grabar" tab.** The in-grid record /
  send / cancel buttons (Android Auto "custom browse actions") are turned
  off. They are handled by the Android Auto app, which can't be tested on
  the emulator, and on a real car the screen came up empty with them on.
  Recording works as in v1.27.0: tap a contact's photo, then ⏸ in the
  player to send.
- **Safer grid loading.** Tile images are smaller (less data sent to the
  car per refresh), and a problem loading the shortcut tiles can no longer
  empty the whole grid.

## 🔍 Unchanged from v1.28.0

Shortcut tiles, unread counters on the photos, phone-style contact order,
the 3-minute recording limit and the approval-request guard all stay.

## Install

Update through Autogram's in-app updater.
