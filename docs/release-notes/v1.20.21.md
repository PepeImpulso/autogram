# v1.20.21 — Android Auto audio routing fix

The "audio doesn't come out of the car speakers" report on Android Auto
traces back to the AudioPlayer's car-detection branch.

## Fixed

- **AA playback no longer falls back to the phone's built-in speaker.**
  When AA was connected the AudioPlayer would walk a personal-output
  fallback list (`Bluetooth A2DP → wired headset → … → built-in
  speaker`). The terminal `BUILTIN_SPEAKER` rung was added a few
  releases back to guarantee "the user always hears something" — but
  in practice it just routed audio to the phone's own speaker while
  the user was driving with the phone in a pocket / cupholder. Net
  effect: the car's BT/wired path worked fine for users who had it,
  and everyone else thought playback was broken because they couldn't
  hear the phone speaker over road noise. `BUILTIN_SPEAKER` is now
  excluded from the AA fallback. If a Bluetooth A2DP / wired headset
  output is available (the common case in cars with BT pairing) we
  route through it as before — that's what carries audio to the car
  speakers. If neither is available we leave routing to the OS so
  modern Gearhead has a chance to surface the playback through AA's
  media stream, instead of forcing the inaudible phone speaker.

## Notes for users

- **If the AA app icon is missing after this update**, force-stop
  Android Auto from the phone's Settings → Apps and reconnect — AA
  caches the app list and may need a kick to pick the new build up.
- For best in-car audio, pair your phone to the car's Bluetooth in
  addition to the USB Android Auto cable. The BT route is what we
  prefer when AA is connected; without it, audio routing is at the
  mercy of Gearhead's media-stream behaviour.
