# v1.27.0 — Record from the car's media screen 🎙️🚗

On real cars Android Auto opens Autogram as a media app (the "History"
list and the player), so the record buttons added to the Car App home in
v1.26.0 never appeared there. This release puts tap-to-record on the media
screen the car actually shows.

## ✨ Added

- **"Record" tab in Android Auto.** The first tab of Autogram's media
  screen is now a grid of your pinned contacts with their photos. Tap a
  contact to start recording a voice message to them.
- **Send or cancel from the player.** While recording, the player shows
  "● Recording · <contact>" with a 60-second progress bar. Tap ⏸ (or the
  Send button, or the same contact again) to send; tap Cancel to discard.
  The player then shows "Sending…" and "Sent ✓".
- **Recordings started on the phone show in the car too**, so the car
  screen always reflects what the microphone is doing.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Recording works with the phone locked.** Autogram's background service
  now also holds microphone access (when the app has been opened at least
  once since the phone started), so a tap on the car screen records audio
  instead of silence while the phone stays in your pocket.

## Install

Update through Autogram's in-app updater. Open Autogram on the phone once
after updating, then connect Android Auto and open Autogram's "Record" tab.
